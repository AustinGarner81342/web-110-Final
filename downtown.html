The Heart of Clayton

Local shops, great food, and small-town charm.

Experience the heart of Clayton through its historic downtown. From unique local businesses to community events and public art, there's always something worth exploring.

Welcome to Downtown Clayton

Small-Town Charm Meets Local Culture

Downtown Clayton brings together local businesses, restaurants, art, and entertainment in one walkable area.

Whether you're meeting friends for lunch, exploring unique shops, or attending a community event, downtown offers a welcoming atmosphere for visitors and residents alike.

Explore Clayton

Where the Community Comes Together

Located along Main Street, Clayton Town Square is one of the community's central gathering places.

Throughout the year, the square hosts events including outdoor concerts and community celebrations. It's a great starting point for exploring the surrounding downtown area.

Location: 111 E. Second Street, Clayton, NC

Restaurants and Cafes

Discover Local Flavors

From cozy coffee shops to sit-down restaurants, downtown Clayton offers a variety of places to grab a bite.

Whether you're looking for a quick morning coffee, a casual lunch, or a dinner with friends and family, there are plenty of locally owned businesses to discover.

Shopping and Local Businesses

Shop Small, Discover Something Different

Take a stroll along Main Street and explore the shops that give downtown Clayton its unique character.

You'll find boutiques, gift shops, and other locally owned businesses offering everything from clothing and home décor to handmade goods.

Shopping downtown is also a great way to support the local community.

Art and Entertainment

Creativity Around Every Corner

Downtown Clayton has a growing arts and entertainment scene.

Visitors can explore the Clayton Sculpture Trail, which features public artwork displayed throughout downtown. The area also hosts live performances, concerts, seasonal festivals, and other community events.

Whether you're interested in art, music, or simply enjoying the atmosphere, downtown has plenty to offer.

Plan Your Downtown Visit

Before You Head Out

Start your morning with a cup of coffee and explore the shops along Main Street and stop for lunch at one of the local restaurants.

It's an easy way to experience some of the best parts of Clayton in one afternoon.

Explore More of Clayton

Looking for something different? Take a break from downtown and discover Clayton's scenic parks, trails, and outdoor destinations.
